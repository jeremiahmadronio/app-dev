## About Movie:
### Gladiator (2000) is an epic historical drama about Maximus Decimus Meridius, a loyal Roman general who is betrayed when the emperor’s son, Commodus, murders his family and seizes the throne. Stripped of his rank and enslaved, Maximus becomes a gladiator, fighting in arenas to survive and gain fame. His ultimate goal is revenge against Commodus and to restore justice to Rome. The film explores themes of honor, loyalty, revenge, and the corrupting influence of power.
